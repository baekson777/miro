# Account Deletion / Unlink Guide

This page explains how to request account deletion (Kakao unlink) and personal data deletion for the **미로미로해 (Miro Miro Hae)** app.

Effective date: 2026-11-15  
Contact / deletion requests: baekson777@gmail.com  
Service Provider: baek son

## 1. What “account deletion” means

When you request account deletion (unlinking), we delete or stop using your Kakao-linked account data and related personal information / game records held by the Service Provider.

## 2. Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open **My Info**.
2. While logged in with Kakao, tap **회원탈퇴 (Delete account)**.
3. After confirmation we will:
   - Delete account/nickname data on our servers (Firestore)
   - Clear related local records on this device
   - Unlink the Kakao connection for this app

**Logout** only ends the session and keeps server data. Use **Delete account** for full deletion.

## 3. Email request (if needed)

If in-app deletion is unavailable, email us with the following:

- To: **baekson777@gmail.com**
- Subject example: `[미로미로해] Account deletion & data deletion request`
- Include:
  - Request type: account deletion / personal data deletion
  - Game nickname (if registered)
  - Information that helps verify your Kakao-linked account
  - Request date

We may ask for additional information to verify your identity.

## 4. Processing time

After we verify your request, we process it within the timeframes required by applicable law. We typically complete requests within several business days and may confirm by email.

## 5. Data that may be deleted

- Kakao User ID linkage
- Game nickname and nickname reservation records
- Game records / best scores stored on our servers (e.g., Firestore)
- Other account-related data retained by the Service Provider for the app

## 6. Data that may remain or need separate action

- Device-only records: remove by clearing app data or uninstalling the app
- Data retained for legal compliance: kept only as long as required, then deleted
- Aggregated/anonymized data: may remain if it no longer identifies you
- Your Kakao account itself: managed by Kakao under Kakao’s policies
- Advertising ID / ad SDK settings: managed in device settings

## 7. After deletion

- Signing in again with the same Kakao account starts a new linkage
- A previously used nickname may become available to others after deletion
- Rankings/records tied to the deleted account may not be recoverable
